3. CANDLE ALIGNMENT
==================================================

This is critical.

Different timeframes close at different times.

For every lower-timeframe candle, the engine must only use the most recent COMPLETED higher-timeframe candle available at that exact historical moment.

Example:

A 5M candle at 10:25 can use the latest completed 1H candle.

It MUST NOT use information from the 1H candle that closes at 11:00.

Prevent all look-ahead bias.
