GOAL:

For upcoming economic events, show an informational "Potential Market Reaction" based on:

- Event type/name
- Currency
- Region
- Affected market
- Existing market mappings
- Existing impact classification

The result must be a CONDITIONAL SCENARIO, not a prediction.

Example:

US CPI
HIGH IMPACT

Potential XAUUSD Reaction:

Higher-than-expected CPI
→ POTENTIALLY BEARISH

Lower-than-expected CPI
→ POTENTIALLY BULLISH

No clear surprise / unclear outcome
→ NEUTRAL / UNCLEAR

Important:

The app does NOT know whether CPI will actually be higher or lower.

Therefore never display:

"Gold will go down."

Instead display:

"Potentially bearish if CPI is higher than expected."
