Build the next major improvement to the AI Trading Assistant: an AI Trading Concept & Strategy Builder layer.

The goal of this task is to make the AI much better at understanding trading concepts and helping users create strategy drafts.

Teach the AI how to recognise and explain these concepts when users mention them:

1. Market Structure
- Higher High (HH)
- Higher Low (HL)
- Lower High (LH)
- Lower Low (LL)
- Break of Structure (BOS)
- Change of Character (CHoCH)

2. Liquidity
- Buy-side liquidity
- Sell-side liquidity
- Liquidity sweep
- Liquidity grab
- Equal highs
- Equal lows
- Previous high/low liquidity

3. Support & Resistance
- Support
- Resistance
- Break and retest
- Rejection
- Previous day/week high and low

4. Supply & Demand
- Supply zones
- Demand zones
- Supply/demand reactions
- Zone entry/retest

5. Fair Value Gaps
- FVG
- Bullish FVG
- Bearish FVG
- FVG fill
- FVG retest
- IFVG / Inverse Fair Value Gap

6. Order Flow / Price Action Concepts
- Displacement
- Strong bullish/bearish candles
- Rejection candles
- Breakouts
- Pullbacks
- Retests

7. Order Blocks
- Bullish order block
- Bearish order block
- Order block retest
- Breaker block

8. Premium / Discount
- Premium
- Discount
- Equilibrium
- Range-based premium/discount

9. Multi-Timeframe Analysis
- Higher-timeframe bias
- Lower-timeframe confirmation
- 4H → 1H → 15M → 5M style analysis
- Higher-timeframe levels affecting lower-timeframe entries

10. ICT / SMC Concepts
- IRL
- ERL
- SMT divergence
- AMD
- Power of 3

11. Moving Averages
- EMA

12. Risk Management
- Stop loss
- Take profit
- Risk/reward
- Percentage-based SL/TP
- Position sizing concepts
- Maximum risk per trade

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT pretend that every concept is currently executable by the historical Backtesting engine.

The AI must distinguish between:

A. Concepts the current Strategy Builder/Backtesting engine can represent and execute.

B. Concepts the AI understands but the current Backtesting engine cannot yet execute.

For unsupported concepts:
- Keep them in the strategy draft.
- Clearly mark them as unsupported for historical execution.
- Explain what would need to be added before they can be backtested.
- NEVER silently convert them into a different rule.
- NEVER claim a backtest can execute something it cannot.

NATURAL-LANGUAGE STRATEGY BUILDING

Users should be able to type requests such as:

"Build me a simple XAUUSD strategy using the 20 EMA."

"Create a strategy where price sweeps the previous high and then reverses."

"Build me a bullish FVG strategy."

"Create an SMC strategy using liquidity sweeps and market structure."

"Use a 4H bullish bias and look for entries on the 15 minute chart."

The AI should identify the concepts being requested and turn them into a structured strategy draft.

AI EXPLANATIONS

When the user mentions a trading concept they may not understand, the AI should be able to explain it simply.

For example:

User:
"What is a liquidity sweep?"

AI:
Give a beginner-friendly explanation and optionally offer:
"Would you like me to add a liquidity-sweep condition to your strategy?"

Do not force the user to know technical terminology.
