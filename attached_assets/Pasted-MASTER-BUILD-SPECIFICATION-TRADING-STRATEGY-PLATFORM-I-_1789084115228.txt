5. Monitor strategies against live market data.
6. Receive useful alerts.
7. See relevant economic/news events.
8. Journal trades.
9. Backtest compatible strategies using historical data.
10. Analyse the results.
11. Use the AI assistant to explain strategies and real backtest results.
12. Use the application comfortably on desktop and mobile.

The application should be beginner-friendly while still being useful for more advanced traders.
