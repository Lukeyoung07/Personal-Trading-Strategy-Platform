GOAL:

Add an informational "Potential Market Reaction" section to economic events.

The feature should explain how an economic event could potentially affect the selected market.

It must NOT claim that the market will definitely move in a particular direction.

It must NOT create automatic BUY or SELL signals.

It must NOT use AI.

It must NOT use machine learning.

It must NOT use fabricated historical statistics or probabilities.

BEFORE AN EVENT:

When an event has not happened yet, show conditional scenarios rather than predicting the actual result.

Example:

US CPI

Potential XAUUSD reaction:

Higher than forecast
→ Potentially Bearish

Lower than forecast
→ Potentially Bullish

In line with forecast
→ Neutral / Unclear

Use cautious language such as:
- Potentially
- May
- Could
- Can

Never say:
"Gold will go down."

Say:
"Potentially bearish for Gold if CPI is higher than expected."

AFTER AN EVENT:

If genuine forecast and actual values are available, compare them.

Example:

Forecast: 3.0%
Actual: 3.3%

XAUUSD:

Potential reaction:
POTENTIALLY BEARISH

Reason:
"Actual CPI was higher than forecast, which can potentially support USD strength and put pressure on Gold."

If:

Forecast: 3.0%
Actual: 2.7%

Then:

Potential reaction:
POTENTIALLY BULLISH

Reason:
"Actual CPI was lower than forecast, which can potentially weaken USD strength and support Gold."

If actual is approximately in line with forecast:

NEUTRAL / UNCLEAR

If the required information is unavailable:

INSUFFICIENT DATA

Do not guess.

STATES:

Use these states:

- WAITING FOR ACTUAL
- POTENTIALLY BULLISH
- POTENTIALLY BEARISH
- NEUTRAL / UNCLEAR
- INSUFFICIENT DATA

IMPORTANT:

Do not show a directional reaction when there isn't enough genuine information to support one.

MARKET-SPECIFIC RULES:

The reaction must be calculated relative to the selected instrument.

For XAUUSD:

US inflation:
Higher than expected → potentially bearish
Lower than expected → potentially bullish

US monetary policy:
More hawkish than expected → potentially bearish
More dovish than expected → potentially bullish

For EURUSD:

EUR-positive economic surprise → potentially bullish EURUSD
EUR-negative economic surprise → potentially bearish EURUSD

USD-positive economic surprise → potentially bearish EURUSD
USD-negative economic surprise → potentially bullish EURUSD

For GBPUSD:

UK-positive economic surprise → potentially bullish GBPUSD
UK-negative economic surprise → potentially bearish GBPUSD

USD-positive economic surprise → potentially bearish GBPUSD
USD-negative economic surprise → potentially bullish GBPUSD

For major US indices:

Strong or weak economic data can have different effects depending on the event.

Do NOT force a bullish/bearish result when the relationship is ambiguous.

For example, strong economic data can sometimes support equities but can also increase expectations for tighter monetary policy.

In ambiguous cases:

NEUTRAL / UNCLEAR

IMPORTANT:

These are deterministic informational rules, not guaranteed predictions.

Create a maintainable reaction-rule registry so additional event types and instruments can be added later without rewriting the system.

IMPACT VS REACTION:

Keep the existing impact classification completely separate from directional reaction.

Example:

Impact:
HIGH

Classification:
Application rule

Potential XAUUSD reaction:
POTENTIALLY BEARISH

These are different things.

HIGH impact does NOT mean bearish.

HIGH impact only means the event may be important to traders.

REACTION REASON:

Every directional classification must have a short explanation.

Example:

"Actual CPI was higher than forecast."

Then explain the potential market relationship cautiously.
