TRADEX — STRATEGY BUILDER UI SIMPLIFICATION PASS

I want you to simplify and reorganise the Strategy Builder again.

DO NOT add more features.

The problem is now usability: there is too much information visible at once, the sections aren't visually clear enough, and the Builder is still confusing for a beginner.

The goal is to make it immediately obvious:

1. What section I am in
2. What I am currently setting up
3. What I need to select
4. What I have already added
5. What I need to do next

Keep the existing Strategy Builder functionality and Backtesting integration. This is primarily a UX/UI restructuring and simplification task.

==================================================
1. MAKE THE BUILDER FEEL LIKE A STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS
==================================================

Do NOT put every option on the screen at once.

Organise the Builder into clearly separated numbered sections:

01 — STRATEGY
02 — ENTRY
03 — CONFIRMATION
04 — EXIT
05 — RISK MANAGEMENT
06 — REVIEW

Each section should look clearly different from the others.

Use a large section number and title.

Example:

01
STRATEGY
Set up the basic information for your strategy.

[section content]

Then:

02
ENTRY
Define what needs to happen before entering a trade.

[section content]

This should make it extremely obvious where one section ends and the next begins.

==================================================
2. USE COLLAPSIBLE / DROPDOWN SECTIONS
==================================================

Use dropdown/accordion behaviour heavily.

Only the section currently being worked on should be expanded by default.

For example:

▼ 01 STRATEGY
   Strategy information

▶ 02 ENTRY
▶ 03 CONFIRMATION
▶ 04 EXIT
▶ 05 RISK MANAGEMENT
▶ 06 REVIEW

When I finish Strategy and open Entry, the Strategy section can collapse.

This dramatically reduces scrolling.

The user should NOT be presented with the entire Builder expanded at once.
