- 7 days
- 30 days
- 90 days
- 6 months
- 1 year
- Multi-year ranges
- Custom ranges
- Pagination
- Chunking
- Duplicate removal
- Chronological ordering
- Partial-data detection

However, the current BiQuote provider only exposes limited recent XAUUSD historical data.

Do NOT change the Backtesting engine again.

Do NOT fabricate historical candles.

Do NOT bypass the new partial-data validation.

Investigate the best way to provide TradeX with substantially longer historical market data while preserving the existing provider-neutral MarketDataService architecture.

Requirements:

1. Keep MarketDataService provider-neutral.

2. Keep BiQuote as the current live-data provider.

3. Investigate whether BiQuote can legitimately provide longer historical XAUUSD data through another supported endpoint, pagination method, or historical-data product.

4. If BiQuote cannot provide sufficient history, identify suitable alternative historical-data providers that can provide long-range data for the instruments TradeX supports.

5. Do not add a provider just because it claims to have historical data. Verify its actual:
   - Instruments
   - Historical depth
   - Timeframes
   - API availability
   - Rate limits
   - Pricing/free limits
   - UK accessibility
   - Licensing/redistribution restrictions

6. The architecture should allow historical data to come from a different provider from the live market-data provider.

HistoricalDataProvider

without changing the Strategy Builder or Backtesting engine.
