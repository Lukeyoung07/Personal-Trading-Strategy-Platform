Build Step 6 of my trading app: Strategy Monitoring Engine.

Steps 1–5 are already complete. Do not rebuild, remove, or change their existing functionality.

The purpose of Step 6 is to create the engine that evaluates a user's saved strategy against incoming market data.

IMPORTANT:

The user does NOT have a fixed trading strategy yet.

The engine must therefore be completely generic and driven by the conditions configured in the Strategy Builder.

Do NOT hard-code a specific strategy.

Do NOT assume the user trades a specific market.

Do NOT assume the user trades futures, forex, stocks, commodities, crypto, or any other specific instrument.

The monitoring engine should work with whatever instrument, timeframe, concepts, rules, and conditions the user selects in their saved strategy.

CORE FLOW:

Market Data
↓
Strategy Monitoring Engine
↓
Evaluate Strategy Conditions
↓
Condition Status
↓
Overall Strategy Progress

For each active strategy, the engine should be able to:

- Load the currently active strategy version
- Load its configured conditions in their saved order
- Receive normalized market data from the Step 5 Market Data Service
- Evaluate each condition when the required market data is available
- Track whether each condition is:
  - NOT MET
  - MET
  - WAITING / INSUFFICIENT DATA
  - INVALID / UNABLE TO EVALUATE
- Show the current progress of the strategy
- Keep the evaluation tied to the exact strategy version being monitored

Example:

Strategy: My Strategy

4H Market Bias       ✅ MET
1H Market Bias       ✅ MET
Liquidity Sweep      ✅ MET
FVG Formation        ⏳ WAITING
IFVG Confirmation    ❌ NOT MET

Progress: 3/5 conditions met

IMPORTANT CONDITION LOGIC:

Do not simply mark a condition as permanently true.

Conditions must be evaluated according to the rules and settings stored in the Strategy Builder.

The engine must support conditions that may:

- Become true
- Become false
- Remain waiting
- Depend on multiple candles
- Depend on a specific timeframe
- Depend on previous market data
- Require confirmation
- Require a sequence of events

If a strategy condition becomes invalid or the required sequence is broken, the engine must update its state accordingly.

RESET LOGIC:

The engine must support the reset rules configured by the strategy.

A strategy should be able to reset its condition progress when the configured reset requirements occur.

Do NOT invent reset rules.

Use the reset settings already defined by the Strategy Builder.

CONDITION DEPENDENCIES:

Some conditions may depend on earlier conditions.

Respect the order and dependencies configured by the strategy.

The engine should not assume that every condition is independent.

TIMEFRAMES:

Conditions may use different timeframes.

The engine must evaluate each condition using the timeframe configured for that condition rather than assuming every condition uses the same timeframe.

CONCEPT INTEGRATION:

Use the existing Trading Concept Library and Strategy Builder definitions.

Do NOT create a second concept-definition system.

Do NOT invent new definitions for concepts.

The monitoring engine should use the existing concept identifiers/configuration supplied by the Strategy Builder.

At this stage, only implement the monitoring/evaluation architecture.

Create the necessary backend services, data structures, APIs, and frontend state needed to support strategy monitoring.

- Instrument/market being monitored

Design the monitoring engine so it can process incoming market-data updates efficiently and only persist state when necessary.
