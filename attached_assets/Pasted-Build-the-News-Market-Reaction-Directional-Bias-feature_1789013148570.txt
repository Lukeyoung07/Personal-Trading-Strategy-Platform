GOAL:

Add an informational "Potential Market Reaction" section to economic events.

The feature should explain how different outcomes of an economic event could potentially affect the selected market.

It must NOT claim to predict the market with certainty.

It must NOT generate BUY or SELL signals.

IMPORTANT:

Before an event is released, the app does NOT know the actual result.

Therefore, before release, show conditional scenarios.

Example:

US CPI
Forecast: 3.0%

Potential XAUUSD reaction:

Higher than forecast
→ Potentially Bearish

Lower than forecast
→ Potentially Bullish

In line with forecast
→ Neutral / Unclear

Do NOT say:

"Gold will go down."

Instead say:

"Potentially bearish for Gold if actual inflation is higher than expected."

AFTER RELEASE:

If a genuine actual value and forecast are available, compare them.

Example:

Forecast: 3.0%
Actual: 3.3%

XAUUSD

Potential reaction:
BEARISH

Reason:
"Actual CPI was higher than forecast."

If:

Forecast: 3.0%
Actual: 2.7%

Potential reaction:
BULLISH

Reason:
"Actual CPI was lower than forecast."

If actual is approximately in line with forecast:

NEUTRAL / UNCLEAR

Do not create a directional result if there is insufficient information.

STATES:

Use:

- WAITING FOR ACTUAL
- POTENTIALLY BULLISH
- POTENTIALLY BEARISH
- NEUTRAL / UNCLEAR
- INSUFFICIENT DATA

Do not use a directional state when the necessary data is unavailable.

MARKET-SPECIFIC:

The reaction must be calculated for the selected instrument.

Examples:

XAUUSD:

US CPI:
Higher than expected → Potentially bearish for Gold
Lower than expected → Potentially bullish for Gold

US monetary-policy event:
More hawkish than expected → Potentially bearish for Gold
More dovish than expected → Potentially bullish for Gold

EURUSD:

EUR event:
Positive/stronger-than-expected EUR data → potentially bullish EURUSD
Weaker-than-expected EUR data → potentially bearish EURUSD

USD event:
Stronger-than-expected USD data → potentially bearish EURUSD
Weaker-than-expected USD data → potentially bullish EURUSD

GBPUSD:

UK data and US data should be interpreted relative to the currency pair.

US indices:

Major US economic events should be interpreted based on their potential relationship with US equity markets.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT assume every event has a known directional relationship.

Create a transparent, deterministic reaction-rule registry.

The rules must be maintainable and easy to expand later.

Do NOT use AI.

Do NOT use an LLM.

Do NOT use machine learning.

Do NOT invent historical probabilities.

Do NOT invent win rates.

Do NOT claim:

"Gold falls 85% of the time after CPI."

unless genuine verified historical data has actually been collected and analysed.

IMPACT VS REACTION:

Keep the existing impact classification separate from directional reaction.

For example:

Impact:
HIGH

Classification:
Application rule

Potential XAUUSD reaction:
BEARISH

These mean different things.

High impact does not mean bearish.

High impact only means the event may be important to markets.

REACTION REASON:

Every directional result should have a short explanation.

Example:

"Actual CPI was higher than forecast. This can potentially support USD strength and place pressure on Gold."

Use cautious language such as:

- potentially
- may
- can
- could

Never present the reaction as guaranteed.
