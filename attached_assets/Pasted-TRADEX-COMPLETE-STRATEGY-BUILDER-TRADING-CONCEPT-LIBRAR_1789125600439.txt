==================================================

This is one of the most important requirements.

The Strategy Builder and Backtesting engine must use the SAME underlying rule definitions.

There must be one source of truth.

Do not create separate interpretations of conditions.

Every concept must have an accurate status:

✓ BACKTEST SUPPORTED

or

⚠ REVIEW REQUIRED

If the Backtesting engine cannot actually execute a concept, it MUST NOT be labelled supported.

FVG
IFVG
BOS
CHoCH
SMT
Order Block
etc.

DO NOT fabricate detectors.

DO NOT create fake backtesting calculations just to make concepts appear supported.

A concept can exist in the library and be saved in a strategy while remaining:

⚠ Review required
