--------------------------------------------------
1. CONTINUATION
--------------------------------------------------

Define Continuation objectively.

A bullish continuation occurs when:

1. A bullish displacement candle is confirmed.
2. Price subsequently makes a higher high than the displacement structure.
3. Price retraces without invalidating the bullish structure.
4. Price then closes above the most recent retracement high.

A bearish continuation is the inverse:

1. Bearish displacement is confirmed.
2. Price subsequently makes a lower low.
3. Price retraces without invalidating bearish structure.
4. Price then closes below the most recent retracement low.

Use existing executable Displacement and Market Structure evaluators where possible.

Expose:

- Direction
- Timeframe
- Lookback/structure parameters if required

Do not introduce subjective "strong continuation" language.

--------------------------------------------------
2. SUPPORT / RESISTANCE
--------------------------------------------------

Create deterministic swing-based Support and Resistance.

Support:

A confirmed swing low identified using configurable left/right candle confirmation.

Resistance:

A confirmed swing high identified using configurable left/right candle confirmation.

Defaults:

- Left confirmation: 2 candles
- Right confirmation: 2 candles

A level remains active until invalidated by a configurable closing-price break.

Support Break:

Confirmed candle closes below active support.

Resistance Break:

Confirmed candle closes above active resistance.

Support Retest:

After a confirmed support break or established support level, price returns to the level and interacts with it without immediately invalidating the retest.

Resistance Retest:

Inverse.

Expose:

- Timeframe
- Left/right swing confirmation
- Break threshold if applicable
- Retest tolerance

Do not use visual/manual levels.

--------------------------------------------------
3. ORDER BLOCKS
--------------------------------------------------

Define Order Block objectively.

Bullish Order Block:

The final bearish candle immediately preceding a confirmed bullish displacement that breaks the most recent confirmed swing high.

Bearish Order Block:

The final bullish candle immediately preceding a confirmed bearish displacement that breaks the most recent confirmed swing low.

The Order Block zone is the full OHLC range of the identified candle.

Order Block Retest:

Price subsequently returns into that zone.

Invalidation:

Bullish Order Block invalidates when price closes below the defined lower boundary.

Bearish Order Block invalidates when price closes above the defined upper boundary.

Use existing Displacement and Market Structure evaluators.

Do not use subjective "institutional order flow" definitions.

--------------------------------------------------
4. BREAKER BLOCK
--------------------------------------------------

A Breaker Block is created when an existing Order Block is invalidated in the opposite direction and then retested from the opposite side.

Bullish Breaker:

Bearish Order Block is invalidated upward and price subsequently retests the former bearish order-block zone from above.

Bearish Breaker:

Bullish Order Block is invalidated downward and price subsequently retests the former bullish order-block zone from below.

Use the canonical Order Block evaluator.

Price returns into the supply zone.

Zone has not previously been retested after formation.
